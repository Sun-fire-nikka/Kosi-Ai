## Key missingness findings

- **Real hydrology (23 stations)**: `forecast_6h`–`forecast_72h` are 100% null (source provides date-based forecasts only; per policy we do NOT convert them to hourly values). `warning_level` was absent from the bulletin and is enriched for 3/23 stations from the official FMISC reference-level table; remaining stations keep it null.
- **Historical events**: 27 missing cells concentrated in `Date/Period` (year-only events) and `Embankment/Feature`. Left as null.
- **Discharge**: complete but covers only Birpur/Kosi Barrage; no gauge linkage to the 23 bulletin stations, so discharge is NOT joined into station features (`discharge: UNAVAILABLE` at station level).
